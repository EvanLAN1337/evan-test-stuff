Nuclear basics

  • Uranium gives off the odd neutron. A neutron that hits more uranium can split it: heat plus 2–3 new neutrons.
  • Water, heavy water and graphite slow neutrons down (they turn violet), and slow ones split fuel far more often.
  • Control rods, boron, lead and concrete soak neutrons up; beryllium bounces them back.
  • Wire a Geiger counter or thermostat to auto rods to build a reactor that runs itself. Try Experiments → reactor.
