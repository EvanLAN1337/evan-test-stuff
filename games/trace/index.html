How to play Trace

Draw one continuous line through the grid.

  • Start at 1 and pass through the numbers in order, finishing on the highest one.
  • The line must fill every cell, visiting each one exactly once.
  • Move up, down, left or right. The line can't cross the thick walls.

Controls: drag from 1 (or from any point on your line) to draw. Drag back to erase. Arrow keys draw too, Backspace steps back, Ctrl+Z undoes a stroke, N starts a new puzzle.

Each puzzle has exactly one answer. The clock pauses while the tab is hidden.
