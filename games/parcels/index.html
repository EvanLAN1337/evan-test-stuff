How to play Parcels

Split the whole grid into rectangular plots.

  • Every plot holds exactly one number, and covers that many cells.
  • The outline around a number gives the plot's shape: a square, wide (wider than tall) or tall (taller than wide). No outline means any shape.
  • Plots can't overlap, and every cell must be used.

Controls: drag to draw a plot, tap a plot to remove it, Ctrl+Z undo, N new puzzle.

Each puzzle has exactly one answer. The clock pauses while the tab is hidden.
